Low mood is an unreliable narrator: it hides the evidence and magnifies the gaps. Let's steady you, gently, one step at a time. Nothing here has to be earned.

Steady the breath

Ready
Box breathing — in for 4, hold 4, out 4, hold 4. It signals your nervous system that you're safe, and slows the spin.

The kinder, truer voice

What's the harsh thing the low voice is saying right now?
Now — what would you say to a friend you love who believed that about themselves?
Your worth isn't a performance review. It isn't earned by output, usefulness, or productivity. You had it this morning before you did a single thing, and you'll have it tomorrow no matter what today looks like.

Evidence · The Worth Ledger

When the low voice says you add nothing, it's lying by leaving things out. Log the small, real things — a kindness, a moment you showed up, something someone valued in you. Read them back when you can't see your own worth.

One small, kind thing

You don't have to fix the whole day. Pick one tiny action — for you or someone else — and just do that. Momentum, not transformation.

If this feels heavier than low — if you're having thoughts of harming yourself, or that you can't go on — please reach out to someone right now.
